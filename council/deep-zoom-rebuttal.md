# Deep zoom — rebuttal

I opened the five prototypes, not only the pitches. Hear paints tan and stays silent until Hear. Draw-and-map shows the sector and its purple image under z², with the angle sentence on the page. Two-planes draws the forest in WebGL (3,136 strings on the wide window) and says that turning does not recompute. The sequence film plays, the purple terms walk, Pause puts the button back, and the picture holds. The disk lesson shows the orange circle and the words “The picture has not moved” until the blank is right. My own page still opens on the checked Mandelbrot window of width about 10⁻³⁷.

## Rivals

### Sound

What it gets right. Pitch is Arg w and loudness is |w|, with a ceiling, so a pole cannot scream. The page is quiet until Hear. The sentence is on the screen while the speaker is off, which I saw: “Silent. Sound stays off until you press Hear,” and the claim still names the point. Escape-as-rhythm is the right handoff. The tick is the iteration count the book already uses to colour a point in §4.2. It is not a new picture of the set.

Fatal flaw on a phone. Three chips, Hear, a compass, and a table of hertz sit under a colouring that already teaches tan. A MAT 412 student in a hallway has no speaker, iOS will suspend the context, and the exam does not ask for 220 Hz. The word “pole” is §7.4. The picture they need is the zero of cos, and the ear is a second encoding of a paint they can already see.

What I would absorb. The tick, and only at the green start on the Julia stop: held if the orbit of 0 is still bounded, ticking if it has left, silent when Pause has cancelled the clock. No Julia chip on `hear.html`. No tone inside the zoom loop.

### Draw-and-map

What it gets right. The purple corner is f of the green corner, checked, and a tile that jumps the cut is not filled. I saw the opening sector land as a doubled wedge, and the readout already says Arg w = π/2. One parser, one clock, and one push of z² + c is named as my problem rather than built as a fourth chip. That joint is the right one.

Fatal flaw on a phone. The first picture is a stamp, not a drawing: the sector is already inked, and the finger still has to manage two planes, three chips, a formula box, and Play. Under 52rem the planes stack. The z window is 7 across and the w window refits (I saw 14 across), so “twice the angle” is a number, not something the eye can check. A scribble of 0.12 squares is the wrong tool for the night before the exam, which is “does this start stay.”

What I would absorb. The cell list, once. One application of z² + c to those corners, exact in the same sense, then stop. The orbit of the blob is not a mosaic.

### Two-planes

What it gets right. The string is one colour at both ends, and the sentence is measured: 0.6 + 0.8i and its opposite land together. I saw that sentence and the forest. The phone budget (36×36, 160²) is stated, turning does not rebuild, and they refuse a volume. They also refuse to pretend a pinch adds samples. That last refusal is the only honest zoom in their pitch.

Fatal flaw on a phone. The hint on the picture I opened says “Scroll to zoom.” Scroll is how a phone looks closer. Their own cut says the paint does not gain samples, so the student zooms into a bigger copy of the same texture and thinks the warp got finer. Drag-to-turn and scroll-to-zoom fight for the same finger. The formula box beside three chips is a fourth control. WebGL here did draw, with readback warnings. SwiftShader is still not the phone in their pocket, and a stalled forest teaches nothing about e^z.

What I would absorb. A link, not a renderer. From one c on the Mandelbrot stop, one step of z² + c can be this forest. The forest does not grow a Mandelbrot zoom, and I do not grow strings.

### Live films

What it gets right. The clock owns t. The hand owns z. I dragged nothing and still saw the contract work: Play, the terms walk, Pause, the button says Play, the picture stays. No GSAP, no second clock, and they refuse to redraw Julia dust as SVG. That refusal is correct. A vector picture of the set at phone size is a lie.

Fatal flaw on a phone. The prototype already puts a scrubber next to Play. That is a second hand on t, and on a 360px row it is the control the thumb will hit instead of the point. The pitch then keeps the minibrot mp4. I have watched that film’s job: one centre, one width schedule, no new c. A student who pauses it cannot move the green cross. The orbit film they want next is the right film only if it stays the orbit, not a second drawing of the set.

What I would absorb. `frame(t, params)` with t from `createMotion` and params from the hand. For the zoom, params is the log of the width. Dragging must not rewind t. The rendered minibrot stays a film of one path. The live page is the path you can interrupt.

### Pedagogy

What it gets right. The picture waits. I saw the orange circle, the green start, and “The picture has not moved.” A wrong blank does not reveal the landing. The 32-step word is labeled as not the Julia escape test, and they refuse to copy it into the renderer. The lock is a chapter-page door, not the router. That is the difference between a lesson and a trap.

Fatal flaw on a phone. Six blanks, Check, and a rule box stand between this student and the picture they came for. The strip on the page already draws Slide and Which c as locked. A phone the night before the exam opens `#julia` from a link in the problem, not from a finished staircase. If the synthesis puts the lock on that hash, the zoom work never gets seen. The rule box under the fold is a second way to set a function the blanks are not about.

What I would absorb. `progress.js` and the inked disk as the sentence on the chapter door: at c = 0 the filled set is the closed disk. I do not absorb a lock inside `#julia`. I do not absorb the 32-step test.

## Shared pieces

One cell grid. Draw-and-map already stores a square as four corners and four images. Deep zoom does not own a pen. The only call I want is one application of z² + c to that list, corner for corner, then hand the fate back. A second grid inside the zoom page would be a second drawing of the same set.

One clock. `createMotion` in `js/core/anim.js`. Live films already wrap it. My Play already is it: Pause cancels, a hidden tab does not advance, reduced motion does not say Pause. Sound’s oscillators arm from that tick or they are a second clock. Two-planes’ damping frame has to be idle before a film on the same station starts. A station gets one of these, not a zoom clock beside a loop clock. I have not merged those two on the existing stops; that merge is the cut I am now willing to make, in favour of the film player.

One progress module. Pedagogy’s `mv:lesson-progress` and `nodesForChapter`. Deep zoom reads whether `4.2.disk` is inked, and only to choose a lede (“you already know the disk at c = 0”). It does not write a boolean, and it does not invent `mv:zoom-progress`. The chapter page is the only place a node is locked.

One parser. `compile` in `js/core/parse.js` is the function. Draw-and-map, two-planes, and the disk lesson already use it. Deep zoom does not parse. The loop’s c(t) is still `path-expr.js`. I will not add a third parser, and I will not block the council on merging those two. A typed f belongs to the lesson and the forest. A typed centre on the zoom page would be a second control for the same point as the green cross.

## Revised pitch, and what I cut

Zoom is one high-precision orbit. A smaller step buys more true pixels, and the page says when the digits have run out.

Cut from my own pitch, after seeing the others:

- The series-skip count in the readout. “Skipped 1429 steps” is a builder’s number. The student needs the width, the digits, and whether the window stayed or left.
- Opening on the 10⁻³⁷ window as the arrival. That frame is the proof. The station opens on Figure 4.6. Play is how you leave it. The prototype page can keep opening deep; I am not changing it in this pass.
- A Julia chip with equal billing. One picture, the set of c, on `#mandelbrot`. The same renderer serves `#julia` one c later. Two chips on `deep-zoom.html` were the proof that both recurrences work.
- A free “factor per second” box beside the step the stations already have. The constant rate is that step, with the two-percent floor so a tiny step still moves. A second number is how the zoom died before.
- Any map node for `deep-zoom.html`. The module is what the two stops call when the width passes 10⁻²⁸. The page stays a draft beside the map.

Still cut, and nothing I saw talks me out of it: a shader, a second reference for every glitch, Newton, steering the centre, and a student-facing promise past the page cap.

## Where it anchors

Not a Chapter 5 section. Sections 5.1 through 5.5 are the exponential, the logarithm, the trigonometric functions, and the circuit. Identity (5.1) is a closed form. A zoom that adds digits does not teach it. The partial sums that become e^z are the forest and the geometric pile, not this renderer.

The first door is §4.2. The Mandelbrot stop, Figure 4.6, page 114, is the wide picture. The orbit of 0 on the Julia stop is Theorem 4.9, page 113: the set is one piece exactly when that orbit stays bounded. The live zoom replaces the fixed minibrot film at that door. It does not replace the orbit film, and it does not take §5.1.
