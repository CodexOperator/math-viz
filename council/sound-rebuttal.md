# Sound, rebuttal

I opened the five prototypes at 390×844. None of them scroll sideways. The notes below are about what a MAT 412 student can actually do on that screen, and about Chapter 5, whose lede on the book branch is still one sentence: "The exponential, the logarithm, and the powers are not drawn here yet."

## Draw and map

What it gets right. The ink is the student's, and the purple corners are checked, not decorated. On load the sentence is already a claim that can fail: "The purple angle is twice the green angle, and the little square only turned and stretched." The three chips are the Chapter 5 pair plus the map the student already trusts, z². The probe is a real export: `picture.dataset.probeRe` and `dataset.probeIm` update with the walk. That is the cell sound should sing, and the pitch said so and the page does it.

Fatal on a phone. At 390px the first screen is the citation, the sentence, three chips, a formula field, and Play. The z plane is a sliver at the bottom. The w plane, which is the whole lesson, is below the fold. The finger draws on a canvas it cannot see the image of, and the formula field is a fourth control that opens a keyboard over whatever canvas has scrolled into view. Six focusable controls on a page whose rule is three.

Absorb. The cell, not the pen. One `{z, w}` from that probe, voiced with the existing `voiceOf`. Not 1800 tiles. The formula field stays on their page. If the compiled id is `exp`, `log`, or `tan`, the beat marks are the known ones. Anything else gets pitch and loudness and no invented zero.

Absorb. One string, the one under the finger: the pair `(z, f(z))` their model already has. The note at the green end is the note at the purple end, because both are w. Do not absorb the orbit, the pinch, or a second audio pan for "which sheet."

Absorb. The contract, not a second sequence film. If a film is playing, the oscillator is updated inside that paint, at the current w, and a pause or a hidden tab suspends the context. No `setInterval`, no beat clock of mine. I will not put a tone in their prototype in this phase.

## Chapter 5 anchor

§5.1, the complex exponential. Identity (5.1), page 132: e^z = e^x (cos y + i sin y). Across changes only the size, so only the loudness. Up changes only the angle, so only the pitch. e^0 is the resting note.

That is the picture the book page names first, and it is the one fact sound can check with the paint covered. Draw-and-map and two-planes should keep the planes. The sound station is the probe on that exponential, not a third drawing of the strip.
