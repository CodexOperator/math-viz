## Sound

`hear.html`. Three chips and Hear. The first screen is a domain colouring of tan z, a green point, and the sentence that w is neither a zero nor a pole. Hear is quiet until pressed. That part is right.

What it gets right: pitch is Arg w and loudness is |w|, with a ceiling, so a pole cannot scream. The claim is a string that is false somewhere. e^z on the axes is the clean check of Identity (5.1): across changes loudness only, up changes pitch only. Design 2.6 already refuses to build a second blank product and offers the claim key to whoever owns the blanks. That is the right split.

Fatal on a phone: the lesson opens on tan z, which is Definition 5.6, page 148, and the bright spot is where cos z is 0. The book does not say "pole" until §7.4, page 232, and the pitch admits it. A MAT 412 student who still needs e^z meets the hardest function first, as a rainbow that is not the four teaching colours, and has to land a finger on a zero whose silence starts at |w| = 1/4, about a fingertip. Hear is a fourth control on a page that already has three chips. In a classroom the phone is muted, and the sentence under the picture is then the whole lesson, which the student could have read without the speaker. The cut of Log is a line. Nothing snaps to it. The same finger that misses the unit circle will miss the seam, and the pitch jump will not happen.

Absorb: the claim key. `grade` compares a blank to that string and does not grow an audio graph. e^z's two-axis sentence becomes blank 2 and blank 3 of the §5.1 lesson. Do not absorb tan as the first Chapter 5 station, the hue wheel as a new colour key, or a Julia rhythm. Escape time already has a definition on the fractal page. A tick rate would rename it.
