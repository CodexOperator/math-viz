## 3. Core changes adopted (C1, C2, C3)

C1 section 6 asks nothing of S6; the new plane API was adopted where it applies:

- `createLabelPlacer` for every word on every plane of both stations (derivative: z0, Arg h, spin, f(z0), the diamonds, f′(z₀), centre, the polar address of A; limits: puncture, Arg z, limit, centre, ε-disk, touches here, z0 = 1). `drawLabel` is no longer imported by either file.
- `sideCands` for `r = …` (`polarWords`) and the far spots of the marks.
- `registerObstacle` for the rings, circles, dots, diamonds, markers, arcs and half-lines; the `obstacle` option on `drawSegment`, `drawRay`, `drawArc`, `drawCircle` and `addCurve`.
- `symbol: 'z0'` on the handle (announcement). **Not used, with reason:** `labelSide` (the handle's DOM word cannot clear the ring on any side, entry 1) and `zOrder` (one handle per plane, nothing to stack).
- `drawSegment(…, { alpha })` for the spin reference ray (entry 8).
- `RAMP_WEIGHT` 0.4: nothing to change; the spokes call `rampColor(base, t)` with the default and follow it. No test of S6 pins a ramp pixel.

C2 section 6 (`specToCurves`, `maxImageStep`, `domainSphere`, `riemannSphere`, `argHelix`, three-view): none of it applies, the two stations import no 3D, shapes or mapping module.

C3 "for other owners": nothing is addressed to S6, and one new option applies: `createExplainer`'s `sameLabel`, passed to the ε-δ explainer (`same radius` for ε, `same comparison` for the "no δ works" case; the footer used to read "same point: ε = 0.5"). The main explainers keep `same point` (A and g are points). `homeLabel`, `showK` and `compact` of the staircase are not needed (three rows, derived home name). `exactAngle` now finds π/18 multiples, so the 10° step of the direction slider reads `Arg h = 10 deg (pi/18)`; every pinned string of the S6 tests is unchanged.

A 6 px offset of the near spots (inside drawPolarAddress's 15 px box) is caught by the unit test only.
