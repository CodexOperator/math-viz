The exponential keeps its angle

ez = ex (cos y + i sin y). The clock owns y. Drag to set x. The radius changes, and the turn does not start over.

A vertical line, while you hold x

Drag in the plane. Distance from 0 sets the radius ex. The clock keeps the angle.

Green is the radius the hand is holding, drawn on the real axis. Purple is ez, walking that radius. Blue is the axes. Orange is the unit circle, the image of the imaginary axis.
