z = √3 + i: length 2, angle 30°
w = 1 + i: length 1.414, angle 45°
1. Turn by the angle of w
2. Stretch by the length of w
3. Land on zw
zw = 0.732 + 2.732i
lengths multiply: 2 × 1.414 = 2.828
angles add: 30° + 45° = 75°
check: (√3 + i)(1 + i) = (√3 − 1) + (√3 + 1)i
