Touch the void. A shape will answer.

    Story Map

    No headset session. The shape stays in the void.

    Enter begins. Space, on the empty void, flashes the centre. Next shows another shape. Arrow keys follow the gesture. Shift and an arrow key turns the sphere or the chordal surface. On the family plane, Shift and the left or right arrow turns the slice, including while the angle slider is focused, and the slider runs from the set of c to the Julia set. The card is a schematic of the angle. Dragging the card turns that plane and does not move c. On the angle sum, keys 1, 2, and 3 pick a corner, the ring marks it, and an arrow moves that corner. The letter c stays put. Dragging a corner moves that corner. Plus and minus zoom when the picture has a window. Enter or Space reads the centre when the picture asks for an orbit or a verdict, and otherwise flashes the shape for that centre. On the chordal distance, a tap, Enter, or Space names the distance, and the orange corners are infinity. On the angle sum, a tap picks a corner and does not flash. A tap on the card does not flash. On the roots of 1, the degree slider steps from 3 to 5, and Lower and Higher are those steps. The question is which root Newton finds. A stripe is one root, painted in two inks. A tap on a basin names the root and does not flash. On the split, the other question is which c keep the walk from 0 bounded. That pane keeps one window. A tap there moves the ring. The basins do not use that c. On the diary, the set of c is an index of Julia sets. The orange ring is this c. Tap or drag it and that Julia set writes back across the void. The colour of that set is how fast the walk leaves. Past the cusp the set has come apart, and the index writes dust. c = 1/4 still settles. Arrow keys move c and do not flash. Enter or Space writes the marked c back. Pinch zooms the set of c. The Julia pane keeps one window. Thread badges sit under the caption. Escape ends a flash. A tap picks any point. A second tap, or Space, or Enter, answers again. A headset is optional. Without one, the shape stays in the void.
