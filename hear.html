One Point, Two Addresses

Hear the point

Drag the green point. Black is a zero of tan, where the note goes quiet. The bright spot is where cos z is 0, so tan runs off and the note climbs. Arrow keys move the point once the picture is focused.

Silent. Sound stays off until you press Hear.

w is neither a zero nor a pole. Pitch is the angle of w, and loudness is its size.

Orange is the angle of w, and that angle is the pitch.

How the note is made

The paint is the same pair of numbers as the note. Colour is the angle of w. Brightness is the size of w. Black is a zero. White is where the size runs off.

What the colours are

  • Blue is the axes: where z sits.
  • Orange is the angle of w, drawn as the needle, and heard as the pitch.
  • Green is the point you drag.
  • Purple is the output: the sentence, read from w. The note is that same output.
